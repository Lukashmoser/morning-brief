**TLDR InfoSec: Wiz finds NGINX RCEs, Optimizely CDN hijacked, Chainguard starter tier**

- **Microsoft agent-framework 1.26.1 and 1.26.2 on PyPI carry a credential stealer.** If you installed either, rotate every secret, CI token and cloud identity it could reach.

- **CVE-2026-115731 (CVSS 9.4) in Puppet is being actively exploited** and is now on CISA's KEV list. The patch came out Nov 3.
