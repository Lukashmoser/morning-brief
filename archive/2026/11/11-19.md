**TLDR: GPT-6.2, widespread outages, being known as a good dev**

**TLDR AI and TLDR Hardware: Microsoft's seven models, Nvidia guides $130B**

- **Huang testifies before Congress on Dec 1.** Waymo halted freeway rides in all 15 cities after a California DMV order.

**TLDR InfoSec and TLDR IT: Java4Shell, Optimizely malware, Windows agent controls**

- **Java4Shell (CVSS 9.2) is an RCE in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder, triggered by 64 "A"s.** Confluence, Jira, Artifactory, Keycloak and Elasticsearch are exposed.
- **Malicious WPPConnect npm and Docker releases steal env vars and WhatsApp sessions.** `--ignore-scripts` doesn't block them; rotate any exposed secrets.
- **Optimizely was used to push malicious JavaScript to at least 178 sites.** Infoblox was breached through a third-party SharePoint migration vendor.
- **Update ONNX Runtime to 1.27.0 or later**; malicious .onnx models can leak host files.

**TLDR Dev, DevOps and Marketing**

- **Kubernetes will remove the kube-proxy IPVS backend.** Cloudflare open-sourced its Kitesurf browser engine for AI agents under Apache 2.0.
- **Salesforce saved more than $5M by right-sizing Kubernetes on EKS.** PostHog's decision models let 98.7% of session summaries skip an LLM call.
- **Holiday ads in September are 6× what they were five years ago.** Instagram now allows Amazon affiliate links in Reels and posts.
- **One founder got 146 qualified leads from Google SEO in 6 months versus 5 from AI search.**
