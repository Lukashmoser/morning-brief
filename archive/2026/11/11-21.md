- **J.P. Morgan AM forecasts 10% a year for Bitcoin over 10 years**, against 5.5% for gold. Deutsche Bank started crypto custody on Nov 11.

**TLDR AI: Gemini tool retrieval, Stripe evals, agent swarms**

- **32 Opus agents sped up a NumPy workload 18.9×, against 3.7× for a single agent** (Replit benchmark).
- **In Stripe's coding-agent evals, runs met 78.1% of criteria on average**, but only 29.4% met all of them.

**TLDR InfoSec: phpAfterShell, Muse worries Meta staff, GTA 6 hacker sentenced**

- **PHP fixed 7 critical flaws.** "phpAfterShell" can be chained into RCE on any app that uses file-based sessions.
- **Apache Roller 4.0.0–6.1.4 has an actively exploited unauthenticated RCE.** Upgrade to 6.1.6 or later.
- **Ruby2Shell is a one-byte heap overflow in Ruby** that affects GitHub Enterprise Server and GitLab.
- **Rakuten Securities may have leaked data on 4.47M accounts**, and TfL Oyster photocard data for 265K customers was exposed.
- **Oddity: a Meta Muse tester says the agent signed them up for an $888-a-month insurance plan.**

**TLDR Hardware: ChatGPT writes RTL, Google books a rocket, California subpoenas Nvidia**
