# Newsletter digest — 2026-11-22

It was a brutal week: the Morningstar US Total Market Index fell 8.5% and tech dropped nearly 12% into correction territory, its worst week since 2020, as Anthropic slid on day two. With tech wobbling, Motley Fool analysts named their favourite dividend stocks.

## Motley Fool

**Breakfast News: When Tech Wobbles, Dividends Pay**

- **The Nasdaq-100 is down more than 13% in a month**, while dividend payers have held up. The market's October dividend yield of about 1.1% was the lowest in 155 years.
- **$100 invested in 1871 would be about $168K from price gains alone**, and almost $114M with dividends reinvested.
- **Picks: Domino's, GE Aerospace, Cisco and AbbVie.** GE has raised its dividend 68% since April 2024, and its 5-year price target is $562.78 (about 11% a year).
- **Cisco yields about 1.7% and has raised its payout 15 years running.** Revenue rose 24% to $18.5B last quarter, and its hyperscaler AI forecast doubled to $9B.
- **AbbVie yields about 2.5% after 50+ years of increases.** The analyst expects Pfizer (about 6% yield, 83% payout) to cut its dividend in the next few years.

## Morningstar

**Yields Are Up. Should You Stay in Cash or Lock 'Em In?** (Smart Investor)

- **The Morningstar US Total Market Index fell 8.5% on the week**, and 93% of covered US stocks were down. Neoclouds Nebius and Iren were among the worst performers.
- **Anthropic (ANT) fell on its second day**, despite strong Nvidia earnings. GMO warns that investors may sell other stocks to buy Anthropic and, later, OpenAI.
- **US markets are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving.** Friday stocks close at 1 p.m. EST and bonds at 2 p.m. Dell, HP and Workday report Tuesday.
- **Rising rates have lifted short-term cash yields**, but Morningstar warns that cash carries its own risks.
