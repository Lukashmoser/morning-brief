## Motley Fool

**Breakfast News: 3 Options Moves for a Jumpy Market**

An options primer; all figures are hypothetical examples.

- 39% of the S&P 500 sits in ten companies, most spending heavily on AI.
- Sell a put: $150 premium on a $40 strike (stock at $50) cuts your cost to $38.50 if assigned; you still pay $40 if it drops to $30.
- Sell a covered call: $900 for a $330 strike on a $300 stock means an effective $339 sale price, with gains above $330 forfeited.
- Buy a protective put: $800 for a $180 strike on a $200 stock caps the loss at $2,800 instead of $8,000 if it falls to $120 — about 4% of the position for six months.
- Option premiums rise when markets are jumpy, which favours sellers over buyers right now.
